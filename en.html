Biography

Dr. Jiang Li is currently a Research Assistant Professor at the Institute of Cell and Gene Technology, Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology. He received his Ph.D. in Biomedical Sciences from the City University of Hong Kong in 2024, with a visiting scholarship at the City of Hope National Medical Center in the United States. His research focuses on the core framework of artificial intelligence and multi-omics big data, conducting systematic studies on cancer precision subtyping, non-coding RNA regulatory mechanisms, and clinical translation. He is dedicated to developing bioinformatics tools and pan-cancer databases for tumor diagnosis and prognosis. He has published 11 SCI and conference papers as first author or corresponding author (including co-authorship) in journals such as Nucleic Acids Research (2020, 2026), iScience (2025), Clinical & Experimental Allergy (2026), International Immunopharmacology (2025), Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal (2022, 2024), and STAR Protocols (2025). He also serves as a reviewer for MedComm and other journals.
