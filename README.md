## About Me

I am a Research Assistant Professor at the Institute of Cell and Gene Technology, Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology. My research focuses on leveraging **artificial intelligence** and **multi-omics big data** to study cancer precision subtyping, non-coding RNA regulatory mechanisms, and clinical translation. I develop bioinformatics tools and pan-cancer databases for tumor diagnosis and prognosis.
