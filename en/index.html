About

Dr. Jiang Li is currently a Research Assistant Professor at the Institute of Cell and Gene Technology, Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology. He received his Ph.D. in Biomedical Sciences from the City University of Hong Kong in 2024, with a visiting scholarship at the City of Hope National Medical Center in the United States. His research focuses on the core framework of applied artificial intelligence and multi-omics big data, conducting systematic studies on cancer precision subtyping, non-coding RNA regulatory mechanisms, and clinical translation. He is dedicated to developing bioinformatics tools and pan-cancer databases for tumor diagnosis and prognosis. He has published 11 SCI and conference papers as first author or corresponding author (including co-authorship) in journals such as Nucleic Acids Research (2020, 2026), iScience (2025), Clinical & Experimental Allergy (2026), International Immunopharmacology (2025), Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal (2022, 2024), and STAR Protocols (2025). He also serves as a reviewer for MedComm and other journals.

Research

Cancer subtyping

Developing and benchmarking machine-learning methods for molecular subtypes and prognostic models that link transcriptomes to clinical response and patient outcomes.

Non-coding RNA regulatory mechanisms

Reconstructing subtype-specific regulatory networks across lncRNAs, miRNAs and piRNAs, identifying master regulators and their roles in tumour initiation and treatment response.

Bioinformatics databases and tools

Turning analysis pipelines into accessible databases, R packages and web platforms — ncRNA-eQTL, PCsRNAdb, GCclassifier and others — for the community and clinical research.
